Emend is a set of AI editing components and editor starters for Tiptap. It gives
readers a clear review step before an AI-generated change becomes part of their
document.

<Callout type="warn" title="Pre-release documentation">
  Emend is still being prepared for its first public preview. The registry is in
  place, but `@emend/ai` is not published to npm yet.
</Callout>

## What Emend gives you

- **Three AI surfaces:** a selection-aware Bubble Menu, a Composer for focused
  prompts, and a document-aware Side Chat.
- **Reviewable edits:** Ask requests return answers, while Edit requests create
  proposals that stay separate until the user accepts them.
- **Two ways to start:** add one surface to an editor you already own, or copy a
  complete editor starter into your app.

## What stays yours

The editor, copied component source, server route, provider credentials,
authentication, persistence, and deployment remain inside your application.
Emend does not replace your product's permissions, content model, or backend.
