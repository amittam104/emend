---
title: Content and Proposals
description: How emend sends your text to the AI and safely applies the change it suggests.
---

A **proposal** is the change the AI suggests. This page explains how emend
turns your text into something the AI can read, and how it makes sure a
proposal is safe to apply.

Your editor stores the document as Tiptap JSON. The AI works better with
Markdown, so emend sends Markdown to the AI and turns the answer back into
Tiptap content. Your stored document stays Tiptap JSON.

## Step 1: emend takes a snapshot

When you send a request, emend saves:

- the text the AI may change, and the text it may read;
- that text as Markdown;
- a version number of the document at that moment;
- what kinds of content your editor supports (for example, tables or images).

If the text you want to change contains something that cannot be turned into
Markdown, the request is blocked. The same is true for the text the AI reads,
unless your code chooses to send it as plain text instead.

## Step 2: emend checks the answer

When the AI finishes, emend checks whether your editor can display the answer.
There are three possible results:

| Result                | What it means                                                                              |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `supported-markdown`  | The answer fits your editor. You can accept it.                                            |
| `plain-text-fallback` | The answer can only be applied as plain text, and your code has allowed that.              |
| `blocked`             | Your editor cannot display the answer. You can still read and copy it, but not apply it.   |

## Step 3: you see a preview

The highlighted text and the suggested change are only drawn on top of your
editor. They are not saved into your document. They disappear when you accept,
reject, or close the suggestion.

## Out-of-date suggestions cannot be accepted

Each suggestion remembers the document version from Step 1. If the document
changes before you accept, the suggestion is out of date and emend will not
apply it. Run the request again on the current text instead.

## Accept is one change you can undo

When you click **Accept**, emend checks everything one last time and then
replaces the text in a single step. Press <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> on Mac or
<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> on Windows and Linux to undo it, like any other
edit. If a suggestion would replace a whole document that is not empty, emend
asks you to confirm first.

**Reject** removes the suggestion and leaves your document as it was.

See [Supported Markdown and Compatibility](/docs/supported-markdown-and-compatibility)
for the content emend can send to the AI.
