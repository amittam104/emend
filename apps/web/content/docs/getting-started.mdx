## Try it here

Select some text in the editor below. The Bubble Menu opens next to it. Pick an
action and watch the AI suggest a change. This demo uses free models through
OpenRouter.

## What happens when you use it

1. The AI's suggested change shows up as a preview. Your text is not changed
   yet.
2. Click **Accept** to apply it. You can undo it like any other edit.
3. Click **Reject** to throw it away. Your text stays as it was.
